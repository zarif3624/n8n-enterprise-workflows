# Enterprise workflow catalog

The catalog is organized by the team that owns the business outcome, not by the
vendor node used to implement it. Each workflow begins as a credential-free
decision service so teams can test policy logic safely before connecting
systems of record.

| Department | Workflow | Primary metric | Typical production adapters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Finance | Invoice exception triage | Manual review minutes avoided | SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Coupa, Slack |
| Human Resources | Employee access request triage | Access-request cycle time | Workday, Okta, Entra ID, ServiceNow |
| Information Technology | Service desk priority routing | Mean time to assignment | ServiceNow, Jira Service Management, PagerDuty |
| Security | Phishing report triage | Time to analyst triage | Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, SIEM, SOAR |
| Sales | Enterprise lead routing | Speed to qualified lead | Salesforce, HubSpot, routing and enrichment tools |
| Marketing | Campaign lead compliance gate | Compliant leads processed | Marketing automation, CRM, consent platform |
| Customer Success | Customer risk escalation | At-risk ARR engaged | Customer success platform, CRM, support desk |
| Legal | Contract intake routing | Time to first legal review | CLM, e-signature, ticketing, document storage |
| Procurement | Vendor risk intake | Time to correct review path | Procurement suite, GRC, security questionnaires |
| Operations | Major incident stakeholder brief | Time to first stakeholder update | Incident management, Slack, Teams, status page |
